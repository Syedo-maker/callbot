# 0.5 — Legal & Compliance Sign-off Pack

For the university's **legal office**. It summarises what CallBot will do and the wording it will use, and asks for a decision on each point. Background and sources are in [06-legal-compliance.md](../06-legal-compliance.md).

> This pack is engineering guidance prepared for review. It is not legal advice.

## A. What the system does (one paragraph for the reviewer)

An automated voice assistant ("Sara" or "Ali" from the admissions office) calls applicants who have been offered admission. It asks whether they will take admission and, if not, why. Calls last about 1–2 minutes. They are recorded and transcribed for accuracy checks, and outcomes are written to spreadsheets for the admissions office. About 90% of numbers are Pakistani and about 10% are abroad (mainly Gulf and UK). Calls go through a licensed Pakistani operator using the university's own number.

## D. Consent clause for the application form

**English**
> By submitting this application, I agree that {University} may contact me by phone, including through an automated assistant, about my admission. Calls may be recorded for quality and record-keeping. I can ask not to be called at any time.

**Urdu**
> یہ درخواست جمع کروا کر میں اس بات سے اتفاق کرتا/کرتی ہوں کہ {University} میرے داخلے کے سلسلے میں مجھ سے فون پر رابطہ کر سکتی ہے، بشمول خودکار اسسٹنٹ کے ذریعے۔ معیار اور ریکارڈ کے لیے کالز ریکارڈ کی جا سکتی ہیں۔ میں کسی بھی وقت کال نہ کرنے کی درخواست کر سکتا/سکتی ہوں۔
